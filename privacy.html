Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

01 Introduction

GS Consultants ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and safeguard information when you visit our website gsconsultants.in or engage our CA, CS and legal advisory services.

By using this website or submitting your details to us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

02 Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide — your name, phone number, email address, company details and the content of your message when you use our contact form, request a consultation, or communicate with us.
  • Information shared during engagement — financial, tax, corporate or legal documents you provide for us to deliver our services.
  • Technical information — device, browser, IP address and pages visited, collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies.

03 How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To deliver the professional services you have engaged us for.

  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

04 Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to make it work, and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some functionality.

05 Confidentiality & Data Sharing

As a professional firm, we treat client information with strict confidentiality in line with our professional ethics and duties. We do not sell your data. We share information only with trusted service providers (for example, hosting or communication tools) who help us operate, or where we are required to by law or by a competent authority.

06 Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described here or to meet legal and professional record-keeping requirements.

07 Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
